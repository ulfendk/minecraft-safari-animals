# Changelog

## [0.9.0] - 2026-10-24
Bears and a jaguar (Bedrock and Java):
- Grizzly bear: big, brown and silver-tipped, with a shoulder hump. Lives in taiga and forests, catches fish and loves honey and berries. Come close and it rears up on its hind legs and roars; stay right next to it and it charges.
- Spectacled bear: black with cream "glasses", a shy fruit-eating climber of the jungle.
- Sun bear: the smallest bear, with a golden patch on its chest. Climbs, likes honey and is up at night.
- Sloth bear: shaggy, with a white V on its chest and a long pale snout. Lives on the savanna, is up at night and attacks if you walk right up to it.
- All bears rear up and roar as a warning when you come close.
- Jaguar: a stocky jungle and swamp cat with dark-centred rosettes. About one in six is black. Its bite goes straight through the shell of a hiding turtle or tortoise, and it hunts capybaras, caimans, turtles and monkeys.
- Spectacled caiman: a small crocodilian of jungle rivers and swamps, with a bony ridge between its eyes. It eats fish and frogs and leaves people alone unless they bother it. Jaguars hunt it.
- New habitat: taiga. Website: a Bears filter.
- Works with Realistic Hunting: the new animals are game too.

## [0.8.0] - 2026-10-20
Works with Realistic Hunting (Bedrock and Java):
- With Realistic Hunting 0.3.0 or later installed too, every safari animal is game. Each has organs in its own model, a weight, a minimum calibre and a trophy. A broadside heart shot is perfect, and elephants, hippos and rhinos need a .338.
- Spooked and wounded animals flee from hunters. A downed animal lies on its side as a carcass until it's field dressed for its meat and hide or rots away, and the safari behaviours leave it alone.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-10-19
Bigger animals and new snakes (Bedrock and Java):
- The large animals are now properly large: elephants (1.7×), giraffes (1.5×), rhinos and hippos (1.45×), crocodiles and anacondas (1.4–1.5×), gorillas and tigers (1.3×), lions (1.25×), and slightly bigger ostriches, wildebeest, zebras, hyenas, leopards and river dolphins. Hitboxes and riding seats grow with them.
- The green anaconda now wraps itself around its prey: it coils up around the victim, holds it so it can barely move and squeezes it every second until it escapes or dies.
- King cobra: the longest venomous snake. When you come close it rears up, spreads its hood and growls. It hunts other snakes.
- Black mamba: Africa's fastest snake, grey with an inky-black mouth that it gapes as a warning. Fast, repeated venomous bites.
- Honey badgers now hunt venomous snakes (they are immune to the venom).

## [0.6.0] - 2026-10-18
15 new animals (Bedrock and Java):
- Honey badger (fearless and immune to poison), porcupine (quills hurt attackers), Tasmanian devil.
- Amazon: piranha (schools, frenzy on hurt swimmers), electric eel (stunning shock), Amazon river dolphin (surfaces to breathe), green anaconda (constricts), tarantula and giant centipede (climb walls, venomous), poison dart frog (three colour morphs, poisonous to touch), capybara, harpy eagle.
- Tortoise and river turtle: pull into their shells when hurt and take much less damage.
- Chameleon: changes colour to match what it stands on.
- Blowgun (pusterør) with darts. Wipe a dart on a poison dart frog to make a poison dart.
- Website: clear guidance on which edition to download (Bedrock for iPad, phones, consoles and Windows; Java for Mac & PC).

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-18
Java Edition:
- Safari Animals is now also a Fabric mod for Minecraft Java 26.3 (needs Fabric API and GeckoLib). It has the same 24 animals, models, textures, animations, items, weapons, blocks and world generation as the Bedrock add-on.
- Java-native extras: the flint spear sticks in the ground like a trident; the club swings slower and the knife faster; the safari hat is a real helmet.
- Field Guide on Java: hover over an animal's name in chat to read about it.
- The release includes the Java jar. CI builds it and runs its game tests on every push.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-18
Stone age weapons:
- Flint Spear (flint and sticks): a melee weapon that you can also hold to charge and throw. Thrown spears land as an item and can be picked up again; sometimes the flint head breaks.
- Stone Club (cobblestone and sticks): hits hard.
- Flint Knife (flint and a stick): light and quick.
- Sling (string and leather): shoots pebbles. Pebbles come from cobblestone (4) or gravel (2) and can also be thrown by hand.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-18
More lifelike animals:
- All textures have baked shading for more depth: lighter on top, darker down the sides and underneath.
- Elephant: new model with large rounded African ears, a domed forehead, a three-part curling trunk, curved tusks, a sagging belly and wrinkled skin.
- Giraffe: neck that tapers from a thick base, sloping back, longer head, tufted ossicones, and reticulated patches with a fine cream network.
- Wildebeest: long dark face with a broad muzzle, horns that curve out, down and up, a beard and a neck mane.
- Vulture: hunched standing pose, pale ruff, bare head, hooked beak, banded wings that spread fully in flight.
- Leopard: clear rosettes.
- Baboon: long bare muzzle, heavy brow, amber eyes and a pale shoulder mane.
- Meerkat: slimmer body and a long pointed snout.
- Smaller fixes: wild dog ears, rhino horn, warthog tusks and mane, hyena mane, zebra ear tips and dorsal stripe.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-18
- Lions are fiercer: a bulkier redesign with a dark mane, snarl and fangs. Adults roar a warning, then attack players who come within 7 blocks.
- Termite mounds grow in savannas and badlands. Meerkats hide in them when their sentry raises the alarm.
- Watering holes: shallow muddy ponds in savannas.
- Safari hat, a wearable pith helmet (leather and wheat).
- Wildebeest migrate across the savanna in herds by day.
- Hyenas grab meat from other hunters' kills, cackling as they come.
- Chimpanzees throw fruit at anyone who hurts one of their group.
- Leopards climb into trees to rest during the day.
- Fixed and improved eyes on the crocodile, meerkat, gorilla and cheetah.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-18
- 24 animals: lion, tiger, leopard, cheetah, hyena, African wild dog, elephant, rhino, hippo, giraffe, zebra, gazelle, wildebeest, warthog, ostrich, flamingo, vulture, crocodile, meerkat, gorilla, chimpanzee, baboon, kangaroo and koala.
- Predators hunt, prey flees, and animals keep day and night routines.
- Ride tamed zebras, ostriches and elephants. Elephants carry a chest and a passenger.
- Thorn bushes: a barrier that land animals can't cross. They grow in savannas and can be crafted.
- Safari Field Guide that records the animals you spot.
- Items: game meat, ostrich eggs (place one to hatch it, or fry it), zebra hide.
